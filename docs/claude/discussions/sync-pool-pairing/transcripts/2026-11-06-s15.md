**What was wrong:** sync paired code and other un-id'd cells only within one slide group. A cell moved into another group or subslide therefore showed up as a deletion in one group and a new cell in the other. Neither could be applied, so sync stopped on a question that had no answer, even when the cell was unchanged.

**The fix:**
- **New mechanical step, `mirror_pool_move`.** Sync now copies such a move to the other half automatically. It only does so when the move is proven without guessing which cell is which:
  - the cell's recorded content appears exactly once, in its new group;
  - if the other half edited the cell where it was, that edit comes along with the move;
  - if the moving half also edited the cell, sync accepts the move only when that cell is the single missing and single new cell of its kind on that half. Then "moved and edited" and "deleted, plus a new cell added there" give the same result, so nothing can be lost.
