   backlog. Four design questions, none decided: (a) backfilling provenance

   PythonCourses topic_0360_rag_introduction (owns the .drawio sources) and
   topic_0400_vector_embeddings (needs the renders in courses that lack
   rag_introduction); static copies caused 48 last-writer-won conflicts when
   both sat in one section. Questions: topic-level versus spec-level
   declaration; whether the lighter step (`<include>` of a diagram SOURCE,
   rendered by the consumer's normal pipeline) is enough; whether
   `clm validate` should warn on same-name different-content `img/` files.
   Check the output-write registry semantics (memory
   project_output_write_dedup.md) and the #664 img-generated split
   (project_664_img_generated_migration.md) before proposing a mechanism.

3. #916 — decoupling the schedule from the section-as-week assumption.
   A "sections are weeks, by design" answer is acceptable if written down in
   `clm info calendar` / `clm info spec-files`. The proposed shapes to argue
   with: subsections as teaching days rather than weekdays; a date-anchored
   `export schedule --calendar/--channel` mode; opt-in workday checks per
   section; aliasing the "week" vocabulary toward "section" (`clm release
   section` beside `clm release week`). Weigh this against the cohort calendar
   projection (`cohort_calendar/`) that already treats buckets as a flat
   sequence.
