# Slide-sync issue history: classification

Evidence for `docs/claude/sync-structural-core-investigation.md` §3.5.

**Provenance.** Written on 2026-11-10 by a research subagent that was asked for a
factual classification and was not told the investigation's hypothesis. It is a
reading of issue and PR *text*, not of code, and its rows were not re-verified one
by one. The counts the investigation cites are in section (i).

Source: `gh issue view` / `gh pr view` on hoelzl/clm, read 2026-11-10. Bodies and all comments were read for every row; for closed issues the fixing PR body was read too.

**Selection.** `sync in:title` (103 issues) united with label `area:sync` (72) gives 112. Open, or closed on or after 2026-08-15: 48. Dropped as not about slide sync: #869 and #967 (`clm release sync`), #960 (harvest). That leaves **45 rows**.

**Rows that are not single engine defects** (kept in the table, left out of every count in section (i)): #656 and #787 are collection issues; #959 (agent-task kit refactor), #961 (`slides translate` bootstrap) and #981 (`suggest-sync`, the unified-format tool) are adjacent tooling. The counts cover the other **40 issues** (20 open, 20 closed). Of those, 36 describe a deck shape; #1150, #1188, #1189 and #1192 do not, and carry `–` in columns a-h.

**Column key.** a = a cell WITHOUT a `slide_id` (positional `pos:` key) is part of the shape; b = a cell WITH a `slide_id`; c = move/reorder; d = insert or delete; e = content edit; f = tag/attribute/slide-hood change (includes `lang`, `for_slide`, `vo_anchor`, id rename); g = voiceover *companion files* (an inline voiceover cell is N); h = both halves changed in the same pass. Y / N / ? (unclear from the text) / – (no deck shape).

**"Engine did wrong" vocabulary.** The requested values, plus two I had to add because several issues fit none of them: *wrong write caught by verify* (apply wrote a bad half, the post-write verify failed, nothing was recorded) and *correct work left unrecorded / extra pass* (the result is right but answered rows come back for re-confirmation, or a row is framed a pass late). "Silent wrong result" is the requested "silent loss", widened to duplication, misplacement and unreviewed content banked as clean. The first value listed is the primary one.

**+/- lines** are additions/deletions under `src/clm/slides/` in the fixing PR (whole PR, even where it fixed two issues).
