- **`clm slides sync`: a shared cell (code, un-id'd markdown) moved into another slide group or subslide on one half now syncs instead of stalling** (#1142). Sync paired un-id'd cells only within a slide group, so such a move read as a removal in one group and an unconfirmable new cell in the other: sync stopped on a `verify_cold` row with no answer, even for an unchanged cell. It now recognises the move when the cell's recorded content proves it, and mirrors it to the other half in one pass (new mechanical row `mirror_pool_move`). An edit the other half made to the cell in place travels with the move. A cell the moving half also edited is recognised only when it is that half's single missing and single new cell of its kind. Two related placement fixes: a placement answer no longer carries a cell into another group when a neighbour it anchors on is still waiting for its own move, and an id'd cell moved to another group is placed next to neighbours whose counterparts are known, instead of a positional guess. Byte-identical cells and cells both halves moved still need a manual step (move the cell on the other half, or give it a `slide_id`).
