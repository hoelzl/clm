- **A job can no longer run twice because it looked hung, and a worker that lost its job can no longer overwrite it** (#799 C3 + C5). `reset_hung_jobs` now leaves a job alone while its worker shows signs of life, and a build no longer touches another build's jobs, so a long-running cell in one build can't be re-run by the next. A hung job with no attempts left now fails with a clear error instead of waiting in `pending`, where no worker would ever claim it. Worker status updates now apply only while that worker holds the job and it's still `processing`; a stale result is discarded with a warning, and finishing a job no longer undoes its cancellation. Other builds' stale jobs stay `processing` until `clm db prune`, which still considers every session.
